QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How it works

The difference between a personal plant and a competition.

What is the difference between growing a plant and joining a battle?

For personal growing, you pick the plant species and follow your own container without competing. In a battle, participants receive identical plants and resources and compete on the result.

How much does personal growing cost?

The price and growing time depend on your chosen crop. Available plants and their current prices in Ⓚ are listed on the greenhouse page.

Do I need a subscription to grow my own plant?

No. Personal growing is separate from the battle membership. You choose a plant and pay for its growing cycle.

Can I cancel Gardener+?

Yes. The membership won't renew after cancellation. A battle you've already started continues until its end.
